## What it shows

A two-panel full-width figure. Left panel: the normalized dipole power
$C_1/C_0$ as a function of GW frequency $f$ for three $M$--$\sigma$
scatter values ($\varepsilon = 0.20$, $0.38$, $0.66$). Each model is
shown with a solid line (mean over realizations), a dashed line
(median), and a shaded 95% band. Higher scatter pushes power into fewer,
more massive binaries, which increases $C_1/C_0$ across all frequencies.
Right panel: the effective number of sources $N_{\rm eff} = 1/\sum_a
p_a^2$ vs frequency on a semilog scale, again with mean, median, and 95%
band for each model. At $f = 1.03\,{\rm yr}^{-1}$, $\varepsilon = 0.66$ reaches
$N_{\rm eff} = 5.27$ (median), rising to $18.94$ at the lowest
frequency; the low-scatter $\varepsilon=0.20$ model — which needs far more
sources to reach the same matched amplitude — sits orders of magnitude higher
($1167.04$ at the lowest frequency). The corresponding lowest-frequency
median dipoles are $0.000779/0.005058/0.050472$ for
$\varepsilon=0.20/0.38/0.66$. The figure is figure 3 in the paper.

## Concepts illustrated

- [[concepts/Cl_over_C0]] — $C_1/C_0$ is the normalized dipole power;
  the figure shows its frequency dependence and realization scatter for
  three population models.
- [[concepts/N_eff]] — $N_{\rm eff} \equiv 1/\sum_a p_a^2$ is the
  inverse participation ratio; the right panel is a direct plot of this
  quantity (full-moment definition). The regenerated value
  $N_{\rm eff} = 5.27$ at $\varepsilon = 0.66$, $f=1.03\,{\rm yr}^{-1}$ is a key
  numerical anchor for the paper.
- [[concepts/brightest_source_fraction]] — the spread among models
  reflects different values of $p \equiv p_1$; large $\varepsilon$
  produces larger $p$, smaller $N_{\rm eff}$, and larger $C_1/C_0$.
- [[concepts/SMBH_population_model]] — the three $\varepsilon$ values
  span the Sato-Polito & Zaldarriaga model range; the figure
  shows how the $M$--$\sigma$ scatter propagates into anisotropy.
- [[concepts/dipole_distribution]] — the mean/median split in the left
  panel reflects the heavy-tailed distribution of $C_1/C_0$, explored
  in detail in `wiki/figures/c1c0_distribution.md`.
- [[concepts/shot_noise]] — the wide 95% bands at low frequency arise
  from Poisson shot noise in the small number of effective sources.
