## What it shows

A three-panel figure (one panel per frequency) displaying the probability
density of $C_1/C_0$ across $10^4$ Poisson realizations of the SMBHB
population at $\varepsilon = 0.66$. **The y-axis is logarithmic** (probability
density). The physically interesting feature — the long tail to large
$C_1/C_0$ produced by rare bright-source-dominated realizations — is several
decades below the peak and is essentially invisible on a linear axis; the log
axis is what makes it readable, and it is the version used in the paper. A
**linear-axis alternative** is generated alongside it as
`paper/figures/c1c0_distribution_linear.pdf` (untracked; produced by the same
script before the axes are switched to log). Each panel overlays four
semi-analytic approximations on top of the exact 3D random-walk Monte Carlo
result:

1. **Brightest source only** (blue dashed): $\delta(u - p_1^2)$ — a
   delta-function approximation that ignores all other sources.
2. **Uniform two-source** (orange dashed): $C_1/C_0$ uniform on
   $[(p_1-p_2)^2,\,(p_1+p_2)^2]$, exact when two sources dominate and
   their mutual angle is uniform on $[-1,1]$.
3. **One source plus Gaussian background** (green): noncentral Maxwell
   distribution (Eq. 1src_gauss in the paper) with noncentrality $p_1$
   and background variance $\eta/3$.
4. **Two sources plus Gaussian background** (red): CDF-difference formula
   (Eq. 2src_gauss) with noncentrality pair $(p_1,p_2)$ and residual
   Gaussian variance $\eta_2/3$.

At the lowest frequency ($f = 0.085$/yr, median $N_{\rm eff}=18.94$)
the distribution has median $C_1/C_0=0.05047$ with a long tail to
$\sim 1$. At $f=1.03$/yr (median $N_{\rm eff}=5.27$), the median dipole is
$0.1911$ and the distribution is substantially broader. The one-source plus Gaussian
approximation is nearly exact across all three frequencies.

## Concepts illustrated

- [[concepts/dipole_distribution]] — the four semi-analytic levels of
  approximation to $p(C_1/C_0)$, including the noncentral Maxwell
  formula (1-source + Gaussian) and the CDF-difference formula
  (2-source + Gaussian).
- [[concepts/Cl_over_C0]] — $C_1/C_0 = |\mathbf{S}|^2$ is the squared
  norm of the weighted 3D random walk; its distribution is the central
  object of the paper's Section on the dipole.
- [[concepts/N_eff]] — frequency evolution of $N_{\rm eff}$ directly
  controls the width of the $C_1/C_0$ distribution; low $N_{\rm eff}$
  produces a broad distribution with a large upper tail.
- [[concepts/brightest_source_fraction]] — $p_1$ sets the noncentrality
  in the 1-source + Gaussian approximation and is the dominant parameter
  governing the distribution shape.
- [[concepts/SMBH_population_model]] — the $\varepsilon = 0.66$ scatter
  model produces the high-$p_1$ tail that makes large $C_1/C_0$ probable.
- [[concepts/coherent_vs_incoherent]] — the figure illustrates that large
  $C_1/C_0$ requires a single dominant source, not the accumulation of
  many moderate contributors.
